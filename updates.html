Version 4.0: Liquid Glass & Tactile Intelligence

September 2026

A ground-up aesthetic and functional overhaul introducing an Apple-inspired Liquid Glass design system, native popup replacements, and intelligent case rescheduling shortcuts.

✨ Liquid Glass UI & Theming
  • Apple Liquid Glass Design: Premium multi-layer frosted blur backdrop, saturated color mesh, and floating ambient glow orbs across every page.
  • High-Contrast Dual Mode: Switch effortlessly between Obsidian Dark Glass and Milky Frost Light Glass with crisp deep-ink typography and zero washed-out text.
  • Floating Glass Theme Toggle: Replaced rigid header switches with an independent circular floating widget (`☀️ / 🌙`) pinned to the top-right corner.
💎 Tactile Dialogs & Smart Actions
  • Custom Liquid Glass Dialogs: Permanently replaced browser's native alert/confirm popups with centered frosted glass warning cards with spring micro-interactions.
  • Instant Undo Feature: Deleting a case now triggers a 5-second countdown progress bar with an "Undo ↩️" button to instantly restore records without opening Recycle Bin.
  • Quick Hearing Reschedule: Added one-tap reschedule pills (`+7 Days`, `+15 Days`, `+1 Month`) inside the edit case window for effortless date management.
  • Seamless Page Synchronization: Harmonized headers, navigation pills, and global footer directories across Diary, Clients, Dashboard, Recycle Bin, and Developer profiles.
